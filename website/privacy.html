KAY LIVE SPORT

Privacy, plainly.

This page describes this download website and the current KAY LIVE SPORT app. No account or payment is required by this website.

The download website

This website does not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or contact forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and download links go to GitHub Releases. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. This page does not claim that the hosting service keeps no logs.

On your device

The app stores preferences such as theme, notifications, volume and playback options on your device. The Phone edition can also keep a recent fixture snapshot to display the list before new data arrives. Reset settings or clearing app data removes stored app preferences. Uninstalling can remove them too.

External services

Fetching fixtures, scores, line-ups and video requires connections to external providers. Those services receive network requests and may receive your IP address and ordinary connection metadata. Their availability, content and privacy practices are outside this website's control. Some links open a provider or publisher website in your browser.

Updates and permissions

The Android app uses internet access and requests package installation for its user-initiated APK update feature. The Android system still asks for installation authorization. Downloads and update metadata are hosted on GitHub. A download link is not a guarantee that a device will accept or classify an app in a particular way.

Questions

For questions or problems, use the project issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include passwords, private keys or other personal information.
